Viridis Research, P1: we revised the proposed Lean statements for the last two rungs of a raster version of the "Trees Cannot Cut" bridge. The rungs are enclosed regions = C8 − χ, and its single-pixel insertion step. The modules are now self-contained. A new bit-level model lets Lean's `decide` check all 256 neighbourhood configurations of an inserted pixel. The statements themselves remain unproved, pending sealing.
