Viridis Research proposed sealed Lean statements for two live Viridis OS capacity kernels. In the decision-information bound, the Landauer limit appears as an explicit assumption. Nothing in the statement proves it for any real system, and without it the bound is false. The multi-ring alignment ceiling is restated as four elementary facts about a defined number. We also found that a canon docstring claim, that adding rings always shrinks the budget, does not follow from the theorem.
