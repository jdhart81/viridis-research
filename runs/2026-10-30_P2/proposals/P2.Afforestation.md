## Kernel decision it backs

The Afforestation kernel ranks seeding plans and stewardship levers by seeding efficiency
(established canopy per seed, `eff = C/n*`). It also scores a proposed seed allocation by its
alignment `η` with the optimal seeding vector, and reports forcing debt `1 − η`.
- The cubic law is what the kernel uses to scale efficiency with site drive: doubling the
  drive gives 8× efficiency.
- The `[0, 1]` bound is what makes `η` a valid score and `1 − η` a non-negative debt.

The kernel's runtime identifier and TypeScript source were not among this session's inputs, so
code conformance is not addressed.

## What the statement says

1. For all real `a, σ, Δμ, C, t` with `0 < a`, `0 < σ`, `0 < Δμ` and `0 < t`:
   `eff a σ (t * Δμ) C = t ^ 3 * eff a σ Δμ C`.
2. For every real inner-product space `E` (any universe, as the canon's `Type*`) and all
   `n m : E` with `n ≠ 0` and `m ≠ 0`: `0 ≤ η(n, m)` and `η(n, m) ≤ 1`.

### What part 2 does not say

The canon theorem's name and docstring mention more than its conclusion: "η = 1 iff n ∥ m",
"forcing debt = sin²Θ", and strictness for non-parallel vectors. **The canon theorem proves only
the `[0, 1]` bound.** Following the brief (say what the canon theorem says, do not strengthen),
the proposal restates only the bound. If the kernel relies on the equality case, for example to
report "perfectly aligned", that needs a separate statement. Sanity examples 5 and 6 pin both
extreme values on concrete vectors.

Observation, not part of the target: none of the part-1 hypotheses is needed mathematically.
The identity is a ring identity, and both sides divide by the same `2aσ`. In Lean's division
convention, `n = 0` or `m = 0` gives `η = 0`, so the part-2 hypotheses are not needed either.
All are kept, because the canon has them and they describe the kernel's domain. Dropping them
would be a (harmless) strengthening, which the brief rules out.

## Why this is the right target

- These are the two theorems the brief names, over the canon definitions.
- The two other core results are excluded. `site_prep_halving_sigma_eightfolds_efficiency` is
  kept only as a concrete sanity example. `sower_IB_ceiling` is a one-line rearrangement of a
  premise, and `homogeneous_limit_recovers_fnt` is the Restoration half-identity in another

- Non-triviality: part 1 alone closes by `unfold eff; ring`. Part 2 needs Cauchy–Schwarz, and on
  the local probe run none of 28 one-shot tactics closed the conjunction. The verifier's probes

## Sanity examples in the module (`norm_num` / `simp`)

1. `a = σ = Δμ = C = 1`: `nStar = 8/27`, `eff = 27/8 = 1/nStar` (efficiency is canopy per seed).
2. Doubling the drive: `eff(Δμ = 2) = 27 = 2³·(27/8)`.
3. **Negative control:** `eff(Δμ = 2) ≠ 2·eff(Δμ = 1)`, so the law is cubic, not linear.
4. Halving `σ` gives 8× efficiency (canon site-prep lever) at a concrete point.
5. Parallel vectors in ℝ: `η(2, 3) = 1`.
6. Orthogonal vectors in ℝ²: `η((1,0), (0,1)) = 0`.
7. **Negative control:** the unnormalised `⟨2, 3⟩² = 36 > 1`, so the denominator is what bounds `η`.
