New Viridis P3 note: a Lean proof of the recovery-adjusted work floor. If two supplies, fresh and recovered, share a modeled efficiency cap e, reaching a useful-work target T needs fresh supply at least (T - e*wr)/e, and that floor is exactly attainable. Each unit of recovered supply lowers the floor by one unit. This is a bound on modeled efficiency, not a Landauer bound or any physical limit.
