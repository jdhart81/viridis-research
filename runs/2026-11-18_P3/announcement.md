Viridis Research, P3: we give a Lean proof that, in a loop of N firms each passing a by-product to the next, a flow that strictly benefits every firm exists exactly when the product of the unit costs is below the product of the unit values. One link can lose money on its own and the loop can still work. For two firms this is the Reciprocity Corridor condition. The result holds within a simple linear model and makes no claim about real costs, values or contracts.
