Viridis Research re-checked the math behind the Restoration kernel, which decides whether a planted tree cluster is big enough to grow into forest. A Lean proof shows that, in the nucleation model, there is exactly one critical cluster size, at r* = 2aσ/(3Δμ), and that the barrier height is half the driving energy of the critical cluster. Field parameters and the model's ecological fit are not covered and remain assumptions.
