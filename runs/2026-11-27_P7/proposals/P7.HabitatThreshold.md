# Rationale: `P7.HabitatThreshold`

## What the statement means

The Levins metapopulation model with habitat loss (Levins 1969; Lande 1987; Nee and May 1992)
writes the rate of change of the occupied fraction of patches `p` as

    dp/dt = growth c e h p = c·p·(h − p) − e·p,

with colonisation rate `c`, extinction rate `e` and suitable-habitat fraction `h`. The module
defines `growth` and `pstar c e h = h − e/c` inside `ViridisStatements.P7.HabitatThreshold` and
states a conjunction of five parts, each for all real `c, e, h` with `0 < c`, `0 < e`:

1. **Factorisation.** `growth c e h p = c·p·(pstar c e h − p)` for every real `p`.
2. **Below the threshold.** If `h ≤ e/c`, then `growth c e h p < 0` for every `p > 0`.
3. **Above the threshold.** If `e/c < h`, then `0 < pstar < h`, `growth` vanishes at `pstar`, is
   positive for `0 < p < pstar` and negative for `p > pstar`.
4. **Levins rule.** `e/c < h ↔ 1 − h < 1 − e/c`. Since `1 − e/c` is the equilibrium occupancy of
   intact habitat (`h = 1`), the population persists in this model exactly while the destroyed
   fraction `1 − h` is below the occupancy it had when the habitat was intact.
5. **Monotone threshold.** For `c < c'`, `e/c' < e/c`; for `e < e'`, `e/c < e'/c`.

**The statement is about the sign of the growth term, not about solutions of
`dp/dt = growth`.** It does not state existence, uniqueness or convergence of trajectories. It
only says where the vector field points, which is what a planner reads off as "persists" or
"declines". `c`, `e` and `h` are field inputs; the statement claims no value for any of them.

## Hypotheses, listed

- `0 < c` (positive colonisation rate). Needed to divide by `c` in part 1 and for the sign of
  `c·p` in parts 2–3.
- `0 < e` (positive extinction rate). Needed for `0 < e/c` in part 3 (`pstar < h`) and for the
  strict monotonicity in part 5.
- `0 < p` in part 2 and the lower end of the interval in part 3. Occupancy is a fraction, but the
  statement does not require `p ≤ 1` or `h ≤ 1`: the algebra holds without them, so they are
  omitted rather than added as unused hypotheses.
- `h ≤ e/c` (part 2) or `e/c < h` (part 3); part 4 is an equivalence with no extra hypothesis.

## Hand check of the "check before proposing" notes

- Part 1: `c·p·(h − e/c − p) = c·p·(h − p) − c·p·(e/c) = c·p·(h − p) − e·p` because
  `c·(e/c) = e` when `c ≠ 0`. Correct.
- Part 2: `h ≤ e/c` gives `pstar ≤ 0 < p`, so `pstar − p < 0`; with `c·p > 0` the product is
  negative. Correct.
- Part 3: `e/c > 0` gives `pstar = h − e/c < h`; `e/c < h` gives `pstar > 0`. For
  `0 < p < pstar` both factors `c·p` and `pstar − p` are positive. For `p > pstar > 0`, `c·p > 0`
  and `pstar − p < 0`. Correct. Note that the "above `pstar`" clause needs no separate `0 < p`
  hypothesis because `p > pstar > 0`.
- Part 4: subtracting both sides from 1 reverses the order. Correct.
- Part 5: `e > 0` and `0 < c < c'` give `e/c' < e/c`; `c > 0` and `e < e'` give `e/c < e'/c`.
  Correct.

## Sanity checks in the module (all checked by `norm_num`)

`decide` cannot evaluate real arithmetic, so these checks use `norm_num`.

- Witness: `c = 2, e = 1, h = 1` gives `pstar = 1/2` and `growth` at `p = 1/4` equal to `1/8 > 0`.
- Witness: with `h = 1/2` (exactly at the threshold `e/c = 1/2`) the growth at `p = 1/4` is
  `−1/8 < 0`.
- Factorisation at the witness point.
- **Negative control (part 2):** without `0 < p`, part 2 fails. At `p = 0` the growth term is `0`,
  not negative, even below the threshold (`growth 2 1 (1/2) 0 = 0`). The module checks
  `¬ (growth 2 1 (1/2) 0 < 0)`.
- Negative control (part 5): with `e = 0` the threshold does not fall when `c` rises
  (`0/3 = 0/2`), so `0 < e` is needed for strictness.

## Why this is the right target

It is the smallest statement that covers what a habitat-loss kernel uses from the Levins model:
the threshold `h = e/c`, the equilibrium `p*`, the direction of change on each side, the
persistence rule in terms of destroyed habitat, and how the threshold responds to the two rates.
Stating it about the sign of the growth term keeps it pure real algebra. A planner can rely on it
without any ODE theory, and the P7-T002 proof stays small.

## Relation to the ladder

- P7-T002 proves this statement; P7-T003 proves `P7.SpeciesArea`. The two are independent.
- Connectivity (project P6, e.g. `P6.SerialParallel`) enters this model only through `c`.
  Part 5 says a higher `c` lowers the threshold. **P7 does not claim how any corridor network
  changes `c`.**

## What the statement does not settle

- Whether any real species or landscape follows the Levins model (homogeneous patches, mean-field
  colonisation, constant `c` and `e`, habitat destruction acting only through `h`).
- Any value of `c`, `e` or `h`, or any extinction forecast or time to extinction.
- Dynamics: existence, uniqueness, stability or convergence of solutions of the ODE. Extinction
  debt (slow relaxation after habitat loss) is outside the statement.
- Stochastic extinction of small metapopulations, spatial structure, and patch-quality variation.
