Viridis Research P3: we give a Lean proof that, in the series-cascade model, n imperfect recovery stages leave a virgin-material share of prod(1 - r_i). That share is never above any single stage's share, and it is strictly below as soon as another stage recovers anything. It is also never below (1 - best rate)^n. This is a property of the model only. It makes no claim about real recovery rates.
