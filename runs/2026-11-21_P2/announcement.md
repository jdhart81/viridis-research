Viridis OS's Tempo kernel recommends how often to revisit a stewardship site, using the cost model h/t + n*t. Its earlier assurance was a receipt from a retired verifier for Lean code that is not on file. We now give a Lean proof, checked under a pinned toolchain, of the six facts the kernel applies: one strictly convex cost, a floor of 2*sqrt(hn) reached only at sqrt(h/n), and a cosh form for the penalty of being off-cadence. This covers the mathematics only. The model and its two costs are user inputs.
