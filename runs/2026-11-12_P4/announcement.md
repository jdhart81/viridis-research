We re-ran a pre-registered test of whether wildfires that start inside smaller compartments of mapped roads, water and non-burnable ground end smaller. Northeast Washington, 11,314 fires, 1992-2024. The frozen script, run unchanged on the registered data, gives the published result: about 13% larger final fires per tenfold larger compartment (replicated under the registered rule). The verifier re-runs it too. The study is observational and does not show that barriers stop fires.
