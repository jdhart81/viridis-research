Viridis Research, P1 rung L1: we wrote a Lean 4 proof of Gray's 8-connected bit-quad formula, 4·χ = Q1 − Q3 − 2·QD, for every finite raster. It shows that the Euler characteristic of a pixel set can be read off its 2×2 windows. This is the first step toward proving, on rasters, the topological assumption behind "Trees Cannot Cut". The proof double-counts over vertices, using one shift-counting lemma and a 16-case window check.
