Viridis Research P1: we give a Lean 4 proof that adding one pixel to any finite raster leaves "enclosed regions − barrier components + Euler characteristic" unchanged (lemma L2). As a corollary, enclosed regions = 8-components − χ for every finite raster. The last step follows the crack boundary of a barrier component as a permutation of a finite set, so no planar-topology library is needed. Machine verification pending.
