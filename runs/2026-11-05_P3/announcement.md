Viridis P3 has started. We drafted four precise Lean statements for a circular-economy kernel, now awaiting a sealing decision. They cover virgin input versus recycled supply in a material loop, how recovery stages in series compound, how recovered work lowers the fresh supply needed, and when a ring of N firms trading by-products can leave every firm better off: exactly when the product of costs is below the product of values. These are model mathematics only. They make no claim about real recycling rates or costs.
